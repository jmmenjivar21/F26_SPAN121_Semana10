Estrategias · Conversation survival kit

When you don't understand, keep talking in Spanish

Use these phrases from Semana 1 instead of switching to English. And when you answer a question, add ¿Y tú? or ¿Y usted? to turn it back to your partner.

¿Puedes repetir, por favor? Más despacio, por favor. No entiendo. ¿Cómo se dice … en español? ¿Qué significa…? ¿Y tú? · ¿Y usted?

🗣️ Lee en voz alta · Read-along

Practice the conversation «En la cafetería» · pronunciation, intonation & fluency

Práctica · Repaso final · No cuenta para la nota · Not graded

Read along until it sounds natural

This is practice only: it does not count toward your progress or your homework grade, so practice as many times as you like. Choose a mode below. In 🎧 Escucha y lee, the words turn green as the speaker reads: follow along with your eyes and whisper the words. In 🔁 Repite, you hear one sentence and then it's your turn to say it out loud. If your microphone works, 🎤 Lee tú turns the words green as you read. No microphone? No problem: the first two modes work without one.

Tips para leer con fluidez TIPS
1First listen at 0.7× and follow the green words. Then try 1×.
2In 🔁 Repite, copy the speaker's rhythm and intonation, not only the words. Questions go up at the end ↗.
3Practice both roles: in 🔁 Repite, say Sofía's lines one time and Diego's lines the next. Questions go up at the end ↗: ¿De dónde eres?
4Watch these sounds: ll = y (me llamo, silla), g before e/i is strong (Diego vs. gusta), z = s (Guadalajara, zona), and keep Spanish vowels short and clear: me gus-ta, not «may goosta».
